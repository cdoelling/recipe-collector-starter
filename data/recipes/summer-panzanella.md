## Before we start

Panzanella is a salad that exists to use up stale bread, which means it's a salad that's mostly about bread, which means it's the best salad. The secret is salting the tomatoes first so they give up their juice, and that juice becomes your dressing.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ripe tomatoes (cut into wedges)
- 6 oz day-old country bread (torn into pieces)
- 1 English cucumber (sliced)
- 1 small red onion (thinly sliced)
- 1 bunch basil (leaves picked)
- 3 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 5 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 oz mozzarella (torn)
- Kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Toss the tomato wedges with a generous pinch of salt and let them sit for 20 minutes to release their juice. Don't throw out the juice. The juice is the dressing.
2. Meanwhile, toast the torn bread in a dry skillet or a 400°F oven until the edges are crisp but the centers are still chewy.
3. Soak the sliced onion in cold water for 10 minutes, then drain. It takes the bite out, so the onion stops shouting.
4. Whisk the vinegar and olive oil into the tomato juice.
5. Combine the bread, tomatoes, cucumber, onion and dressing, and let it sit 10 minutes so the bread soaks it up.
6. Fold in the basil and mozzarella just before serving.

## Kitchen's closed

Panzanella doesn't keep. Make what you'll eat tonight and accept that soggy bread is a tomorrow problem.

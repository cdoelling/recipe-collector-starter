## Before we start

Fry your own tortilla strips. I know the bag of chips is right there. The bag of chips is lying to you. Homemade strips take five minutes and they're the difference between soup and The Soup.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- Neutral oil, for browning and frying
- Kosher salt
- 1 yellow onion (diced)
- 3 garlic cloves (minced)
- 1 jalapeño (seeded and minced)
- 28 oz canned crushed tomatoes
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 6 corn tortillas (cut into strips)
- 1 avocado (sliced)
- 2 limes (cut into wedges)
- 1 bunch cilantro

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a heavy pot and brown the chicken thighs on both sides. Set them aside.
2. Add the onion, garlic and jalapeño and cook until soft, about 6 minutes.
3. Stir in the cumin and chili powder and cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant. Thirty seconds. Not a minute. Spices go from toasty to bitter faster than you'd think.
4. Add the tomatoes and broth, put the chicken back in, and bring to a simmer.
5. Simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, then shred the chicken and return it to the pot. Two forks. Very satisfying. Work out whatever you need to work out.
6. Fry the tortilla strips in a little oil until crisp and drain them on paper towels. Try not to eat them all standing at the stove. I never manage it either.
7. Season the soup and serve topped with tortilla strips, avocado, cilantro and a lime wedge.

## Kitchen's closed

Keep the strips out of the leftovers or they'll go soggy by morning. Soggy strips are just sad noodles.
